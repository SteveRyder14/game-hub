Overview

About the game

Get three in a row before your opponent does. Simple to learn, and the options give you a reason to keep playing.

Place your mark, block your opponent's line, and try to line up three in a row. This version keeps that core and adds the parts that make it a game to come back to: AI opponents on Easy, Medium, and Hard, a bigger 5 x 5 board, a blitz timer, and Infinity Mode, where your oldest mark disappears once you run out of room.

Pass and play lets two people share one device. Online multiplayer connects you to someone in another place. The game is written in Python with the Kivy library, and online matches go through a separate server that keeps both boards in sync.

Reference

Rules and options

The basics, plus the settings that change how a match plays.

Board size
Choose 3 x 3 or 5 x 5 before you start. X always goes first.
Winning line
On the 3 x 3 board you need three in a row. On the 5 x 5 board you need four. Rows, columns, and diagonals all count.
Blitz Timer
Each turn gets a 5 second clock. If it runs out, a random move is played for you.
Infinity Mode
Each player can keep only 3 marks on the 3 x 3 board, or 4 on the 5 x 5 board. Placing another mark makes your oldest one disappear, so matches rarely end in a tie.
Undo
Take back a move during a match. The Undo button can be turned on or off in Settings.
Sound and theme
Sound effects and music each have their own volume. The theme is Forest Mint.
