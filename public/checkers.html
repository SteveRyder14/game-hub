Overview

About the game

Two colours, one board, and every move counts. Learn the basics in a few minutes and keep sharpening your play.

Navy and Crimson face off on a classic board. You move your pieces forward on the dark squares and capture by jumping over your opponent. With Mandatory Jumps on, you must take a capture when one is available, so think a move ahead.

The Grandmaster Edition adds the things that make it more than a single game: an AI on three difficulty levels with a record kept for each, a four-tier campaign, a hint button for when you are stuck, and an online mode for playing other people.

Modes

Three ways to play

Against the computer, with a friend on one device, or against someone online.

Vs AI

Play the computer on Easy, Medium, or Hard. Your wins and losses are tracked for each level.

Local multiplayer

Two players share one device. Pick Navy or Crimson to start, then take turns.

Online PvP

Play someone over the internet. Use Check Server on the main menu to test the connection first.

Settings and rules

Make the game look and play the way you like.

Theme
Royal is the theme shown in these screenshots, with Navy and Crimson pieces on a classic board.
Board style
Classic is the default board look.
Visual quality
Set to High here. Lower it on older devices for lighter, smoother play.
Blitz Timer
Off by default. Turn it on to add a timer to each turn.
Show Undo and Show Hint
Both are on by default. Turn them off if you want a harder match with no safety net.
Mandatory Jumps
On by default. When a capture is available you must take it.
Audio and performance
Sound and performance options are at the bottom of the Settings screen.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need the internet?

Vs AI and local multiplayer work without a connection. Online PvP needs one.

What does Check Server do?

It tests the connection to the online server, so you know whether online play is ready before you start a match.

Who starts the game?

You choose Navy or Crimson as the starting player on the main menu before each match.
